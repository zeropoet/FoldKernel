# FoldKernel Product Boundary

## Product thesis

FoldKernel is deterministic identity and provenance infrastructure for systems
that must evolve without losing their origin.

The initial audience is not every application developer. It is builders of
long-lived digital and material bodies of work: generative studios, archives,
serialized-object programs, collectible publishers, cultural institutions,
and instruments whose outputs must remain independently reproducible.

## Available now: open protocol

The current repository provides, under the MIT License:

- the language-neutral FoldKernel 1.0.0 byte contract;
- the Swift reference implementation;
- immutable conformance vectors;
- a local executable example;
- a public integration-declaration schema and Swift receipt verifier;
- a deterministic, non-transferable Value Receipt 1.0 evidence lifecycle;
- a preservation-safe Transformation Receipt 1.0 for consequential change;
- macOS and Linux conformance testing; and
- release-pinned package consumption.

Independent verification must remain possible without a paid account. Existing
MIT releases remain usable under their published terms.

## Future commercial surfaces

These are product directions, not claims about services already available.

### Managed runtime

Execute admitted event histories, preserve signed immutable receipts, maintain
versioned registries, expose bounded APIs, and monitor reproducibility without
taking ownership of an application's source authority.

### Compatibility certification

Run conformance suites across implementations, record the exact protocol and
package versions tested, and issue reviewable compatibility evidence. A
certificate cannot replace independent verification.

### Instruments

Offer separately bounded visual, sonic, material-production, issuance, and
propagation modules derived from lessons proven in the zeropoet.xyz ecosystem.
The existing artworks, brands, private data, and repository-specific authority
do not become generic customer templates.

### Telos console

Map declared systems and their authority boundaries; observe version,
propagation, and verification state; detect drift; and explain what changed.
Telos may coordinate evidence but cannot alter FoldKernel outputs, absorb a
source's authority, or receive private customer data merely because a system is
mapped.

### Architecture and support

Provide implementation design, integration review, migration planning,
production support, and long-term protocol stewardship.

## What customers would pay for

Payment belongs around operational value: managed execution, retained history,
signing boundaries, production reliability, private modules, certification,
support, and integration. Payment does not make a hash more canonical and does
not restrict the right to verify an open protocol output.

## External proof gates

The product thesis is not validated until outside builders can:

1. produce and verify a first artifact without private zeropoet context;
2. build at least three materially different systems, including one outside
   the art or collectible domain;
3. reproduce identities across supported machines or implementations;
4. explain which authority belongs to the kernel, their application, and
   Telos; and
5. choose to pay for an operational layer after using the open protocol.

Until those gates are met, FoldKernel is a credible developer preview and
architectural thesis—not a universal platform claim.
